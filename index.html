Assumptions and caveats

Source of cutoffs
General rule-of-thumb benchmarks commonly used by retail and value investors. They are not from any single official source.
Sector matters
Ideal values differ by industry. Banks/NBFCs: ignore Debt to Equity, Current/Quick ratio and Net debt/EBITDA. Capital-heavy sectors usually have higher debt and lower ROA. IT and FMCG typically justify higher P/E.
Growth stocks
A high P/E can be fair for fast-growing companies – check the PEG ratio alongside P/E.
Dividend yield
Low yield is not bad for companies that reinvest profits for growth; treat it as a minor factor.
Trend check
One year’s numbers can mislead. Compare each metric over 5–10 years before deciding.
